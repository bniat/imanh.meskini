My Master's Thesis, **“Early Detection of Anomalies and Intrusions in Interconnected Systems”**, received the International Award for Best Master's Thesis/Project of the Smart Rural IoT and Secured Environments programme (C056/23-UNED), in collaboration with the National Cybersecurity Institute of Spain (INCIBE).

The thesis was supervised by María Cristina Alcaraz Tello and Francisco Javier López Muñoz at NICS Lab.
